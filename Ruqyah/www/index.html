"Allah — there is no deity except Him, the Ever-Living, the Sustainer of existence. Neither drowsiness overtakes Him nor sleep. To Him belongs whatever is in the heavens and on the earth..."

Amsayna — Entering the Evening1×
أَمْسَيْنَا وَأَمْسَى الْمُلْكُ لِلَّهِ وَالْحَمْدُ لِلَّهِ لَا إِلَٰهَ إِلَّا اللَّهُ وَحْدَهُ لَا شَرِيكَ لَهُ لَهُ الْمُلْكُ وَلَهُ الْحَمْدُ وَهُوَ عَلَى كُلِّ شَيْءٍ قَدِيرٌ رَبِّ أَسْأَلُكَ خَيْرَ مَا فِي هَذِهِ اللَّيْلَةِ وَخَيْرَ مَا بَعْدَهَا وَأَعُوذُ بِكَ مِنْ شَرِّ مَا فِي هَذِهِ اللَّيْلَةِ وَشَرِّ مَا بَعْدَهَا رَبِّ أَعُوذُ بِكَ مِنَ الْكَسَلِ وَسُوءِ الْكِبَرِ رَبِّ أَعُوذُ بِكَ مِنْ عَذَابٍ فِي النَّارِ وَعَذَابٍ فِي الْقَبْرِ
Amsayna wa amsal-mulku lillahi, wal-hamdu lillah. La ilaha illallahu wahdahu la sharika lah, lahul-mulku walahul-hamd, wa huwa 'ala kulli shay'in qadir. Rabbi as'aluka khayra ma fi hadhihil-laylati wa khayra ma ba'daha, wa a'udhu bika min sharri ma fi hadhihil-laylati wa sharri ma ba'daha. Rabbi a'udhu bika minal-kasali wa su'il-kibar. Rabbi a'udhu bika min 'adhabin fin-nar wa 'adhabin fil-qabr.
"We have entered the evening, and the dominion has entered the evening belonging entirely to Allah. All praise is for Allah. There is no deity except Allah, alone with no partner — to Him belongs the dominion and all praise, and He is over all things capable. My Lord, I ask You for the good of this night and what follows it, and I seek refuge in You from the evil of this night and what follows it. My Lord, I seek refuge in You from laziness and the evil of old age. My Lord, I seek refuge in You from punishment in the Fire and punishment in the grave."
Sahih Muslim 2723 — narrated by Ibn Mas'ud رضي الله عنه · The Prophet ﷺ recited this each evening as the day ended

Ruqyah Recitation Guide

The Prophet ﷺ performed Ruqyah on himself using Al-Mu'awwidhat. Recite with wudu, with certainty that Allah alone is the Healer. You may recite over water to drink, over olive oil to apply, or blow lightly (nafth) onto your hands and pass them over the body.

"The evil eye is real. If anything were to overtake the divine decree, it would be the evil eye." — Narrated by Ibn Abbas رضي الله عنه

قُلْ هُوَ اللَّهُ أَحَدٌ ۝ اللَّهُ الصَّمَدُ ۝ لَمْ يَلِدْ وَلَمْ يُولَدْ ۝ وَلَمْ يَكُنْ لَهُ كُفُوًا أَحَدٌ
Quran 112 — Equal in reward to one-third of the Quran (Bukhari 5013). The declaration of pure Tawhid is the foundation of all Ruqyah. Sihr operates through shirk; Tawhid dismantles it.

Step 5 · Refuge from Darkness & Envy
Surah Al-Falaq — 3 times
قُلْ أَعُوذُ بِرَبِّ الْفَلَقِ ۝ مِنْ شَرِّ مَا خَلَقَ ۝ وَمِنْ شَرِّ غَاسِقٍ إِذَا وَقَبَ ۝ وَمِنْ شَرِّ النَّفَّاثَاتِ فِي الْعُقَدِ ۝ وَمِنْ شَرِّ حَاسِدٍ إِذَا حَسَدَ
Quran 113 — Verse 4 explicitly names those who blow on knots (sihr). Verse 5 seeks refuge from the envier. This surah was revealed in the context of sihr performed against the Prophet ﷺ.
🎙Mishary Alafasy — Al-Falaq
Step 6 · Refuge from Whispers
Surah An-Nas — 3 times
قُلْ أَعُوذُ بِرَبِّ النَّاسِ ۝ مَلِكِ النَّاسِ ۝ إِلَٰهِ النَّاسِ ۝ مِنْ شَرِّ الْوَسْوَاسِ الْخَنَّاسِ ۝ الَّذِي يُوَسْوِسُ فِي صُدُورِ النَّاسِ ۝ مِنَ الْجِنَّةِ وَالنَّاسِ
Quran 114 — Seeks refuge from Al-Waswas Al-Khannas, the whisperer who retreats — the shayateen of jinn and humans who plant doubt, fear, and confusion.
